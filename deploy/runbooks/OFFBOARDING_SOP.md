# Center Offboarding SOP

## Purpose

Define a repeatable handover process when a center exits managed hosting.

## Procedure

1. Announce and confirm offboarding window.
2. Freeze non-critical changes during transfer period.
3. Generate and validate transfer pack using `HANDOVER_CHECKLIST.md`.
4. Rotate and transfer credentials to center-owned control plane.
5. Support one restore verification in the destination environment.
6. Obtain formal acceptance and responsibility transfer sign-off.
7. Decommission managed-center infrastructure only after sign-off.

## Required Outputs

- Transfer pack delivery record
- Acceptance record
- Decommission confirmation
